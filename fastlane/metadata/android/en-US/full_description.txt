Manga Loader is an unofficial MangaLib client for Android. It reads chapters, downloads them for offline use and keeps the library in sync.

Reader
- Vertical webtoon strip and paged modes: right-to-left for manga, left-to-right for comics, one or two pages on wide screens.
- Pinch zoom for the whole reader, one-finger panning, quick page jump and a chapter picker.
- Image processing: edge sharpening, white-border cropping and optional upscaling.
- Hands-free auto-scroll with adjustable speed.
- Light, dark and pure black AMOLED themes.

Downloads
- A native Rust engine downloads chapters and packs them into standard CBZ archives.
- Optional page compression, automatic removal of read chapters and cache cleanup.
- Downloads keep running in the background and retry on connection drops.
- Files stay readable in any CBZ-compatible app.

Library
- Sign in with a MangaLib account to sync reading, planned, completed, favorites and dropped lists.
- Local history, reading statistics, streaks and an exportable recap card.
- Custom lists, filters and full-text search.

Updates and desktop
- Notifications about new chapters of followed titles.
- Windows and Linux builds with a desktop layout and keyboard shortcuts.

Privacy
- No ads, no analytics and no trackers.
- The app talks only to MangaLib. Account cookies stay on the device.
